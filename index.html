Fund NAV Initial AUM and NAV units are set per fund

NAV = (net market value of long positions less short liabilities + cash balance − estimated transaction charges) ÷ fund units. Transaction charges are deducted from NAV; estimated ongoing borrowing and MTF interest are reflected in the latest NAV. Short-sale proceeds increase cash while the short liability is marked to market. Required margin is 115% of current short notional and is reserved from available cash; it does not reduce NAV. NAV history uses available price history.

Fees by fund Dhan tariff-based estimate

Fees apply the current published Dhan tariff to transactions recorded in this browser. They are estimates, not broker contract-note values, and may differ from actual debits—especially for older trades, BSE trades, special scrip groups, options expiry/exercise, exchange-specific F&O/commodity levies, and trades whose product type was not recorded. Equity defaults to NSE delivery unless a product is selected on the transaction form.

Charge composition

Transaction-level breakdown brokerage, taxes, DP charges and estimated MTF interest

Rates and assumptions

Official tariff: Dhan Pricing & Brokerage Charges. NSE rates are used for equity exchange charges because the ledger does not store execution exchange. MTF interest assumes 75% broker funding and applies the current interest tier to the estimated funded amount for the holding period; actual funding and slab may differ. Auto-square-off and call-and-trade fees are excluded because the ledger does not identify those services.

Market data source

Fyers is the primary source for current prices and last traded prices, including when NSE is closed. Quotes refresh every 15 seconds during market hours and about every 5 minutes outside market hours. NSE Bhavcopy is used for historical price data, the symbol directory and the downloadable archive only; it does not override current valuations.

Prices are loaded from data/prices.json.
